# Post 01 — Re-introduce (first post, all platforms)

Video: 18s, 1080x1920, 60fps, music bed, no voiceover. Text-on-screen only.
On-screen copy: *Let me introduce myself / My name is Jeyashree / I grew up in
the Triangle, left to live in different places / and found my way back home /
Now I'm your local real estate agent & guide to cary, apex, holly springs, and
more / Welcome to my page.*

**Because there is no voiceover, the caption carries every word of substance.**
The video gives atmosphere and a name. It does not say why to follow.

## Fix before posting

1. **No brokerage attribution on screen.** This is advertising by a licensed
   agent, so the disclosure goes in the caption. Kept in every option below.
2. **Cross-posting audio.** If the track came from Instagram's audio library it
   will not carry to TikTok. Check before exporting.

Town list on screen is as she wants it — settled, do not re-raise.

## Platform notes

- **Instagram** — first ~125 characters show before "more", so line one is the
  whole hook. Every option above fits inside that.

  space is tight.
- **Facebook / LinkedIn** — no truncation pressure, but short still reads
  better on an intro post.
